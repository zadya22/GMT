The problem

A program built to empower — undermined by its own operations

Excellent intent, talented people — but fragmented data systems, changing tools, and a black-box financing pipeline eroded the program's potential for coaches and entrepreneurs alike.

"The system changes every two months"

Coaches re-learn tools with no training, creating shadow spreadsheets and inconsistent data.

"Trained repeatedly, rarely funded"

Entrepreneurs wait 4–8 months between winning a pitch and receiving disbursed funds.

"A meeting that could be a dashboard"

Weekly status meetings exist only because the data isn't visible anywhere else.

Unified data management

One source of truth, stable enough to trust

Single master database

Role-based views

Field team sees relevant columns; HQ sees theirs — without overwhelming either.

Locked core schema

Auto-calculated indicators

Jobs created, revenue growth — surfaced automatically, no manual computation.
